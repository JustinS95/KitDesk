# SRC-01 — Sponsor brief

Scenario date: 2026-10-05. Author: Leon Patel, fictional workshop manager.

## B01 — Problem

Members email the desk to ask whether equipment is free. Coordinators compare requests with a spreadsheet and sometimes promise the same item twice. The workshop needs a consistent booking-to-return process.

## B02 — Candidate first release

Members should browse equipment and request an item for a date/time interval. Desk staff should decide requests and record equipment going out and coming back. Members should be able to see their own requests and loans. The manager wants a view of overdue equipment.

## B03 — Constraints

Use existing workshop membership; no public sign-up. Payments, fines, recurring bookings and inventory transfers are outside the first release. No delivery date, service-level target or measurable success threshold has been approved.

## B04 — Sponsor request

Please explore reminders and reporting, but do not let them delay the core lending workflow without a scope discussion. I have not decided whether reminders belong in release one.
