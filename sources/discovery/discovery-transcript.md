# SRC-02 — Fictional discovery transcript

Scenario date: 2026-10-07. Participants: Maya (desk), Leon (sponsor), Aisha (membership), Sam (technical lead), facilitator.

## T01 — Current work

Maya: Members email us. I look at the spreadsheet and reply when I can. A request sitting in my inbox does not reserve the tool. Sometimes another coordinator answers a different email first.

## T02 — Request decisions

Maya: For launch, I want desk approval on every request. A member submitting a request should not see it as confirmed. When I reject one, the member needs to know why.

Leon: Agreed: staff approval for launch. Let's record that decision. We haven't discussed whether a pending request should affect availability in the portal.

## T03 — Privacy

Aisha: Members can see their own bookings. Another member's name and contact details must not be revealed through availability. Desk coordinators need the borrowing member's contact details to arrange collection. I'm not approving a broader staff directory here.

Leon: Agreed on those visibility boundaries.

## T04 — Cancellation

Maya: Members should cancel their own requests while pending. After approval, please tell them to contact the desk; staff need to manage the schedule.

Facilitator: Is that an agreed restriction for release one?

Leon: Not yet. Let's revisit it with the cancellation screen.

## T05 — Overlap and eligibility

Maya: We cannot approve overlapping bookings for the same physical item. I'm not sure how to treat back-to-back bookings; we may need inspection time.

Aisha: We should check membership eligibility when someone requests equipment. Whether to check again at approval and checkout needs a policy decision. Some tools require training, but which tools and who records training are unresolved.

## T06 — Handover and return

Maya: Staff record the actual handover and return. Members must not mark their own equipment as returned. A return can reveal damage; we currently write a note and keep that tool off the shelf until someone checks it.

Sam: We could model that as an unavailable state, but I need to know who can clear it. I won't assume returning a tool means it immediately becomes bookable.

## T07 — Notifications

Leon: An approval or rejection should be visible to the member in the portal. Email sounds helpful, but I haven't approved a delivery channel or reminder schedule. Keep overdue reminder timing open.

## T08 — Actions and outcomes

Facilitator: Maya will bring examples of damaged returns. Aisha will propose eligibility checks. Leon will decide cancellation and reminder scope. No due dates were agreed. We still need a baseline for coordinator effort and duplicate-booking frequency before setting success targets.
