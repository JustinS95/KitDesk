# FX-SRC-02 — Synthetic member and desk interview extracts

Version 1. Scenario date: 2026-10-21. **All utterances are invented; two unnamed fictional members and Maya are illustrative perspectives, not a representative sample or actual interviewees.** Bracketed FX-R references are author-created extension premises, review pending; see [authority](README.md#authority-and-baseline).

## M01 — Finding the right physical item

Member, occasional borrower: “There are two drills that look almost the same. I need to know which actual item I'm reserving, and whether the carrying case is part of it. A category name alone isn't enough.”

Member, frequent borrower: “I usually know the item label from the workshop. Sometimes I only know ‘drill’. I want to look by name or item label and narrow the list by category. If nothing matches, tell me that rather than making the equipment look permanently unavailable.”

**FX-R01:** for extension discovery exercises, members can browse physical item summaries, search by equipment name or item identifier and narrow by category. A result identifies the individually lendable item, its description and listed included parts. Opening the item exposes the same identity, not an interchangeable category booking. A completed search with no matching items has a distinct no-results outcome; inability to obtain the catalog is not represented as zero equipment. Search matching, sorting, category taxonomy, spelling assistance and field limits are not specified. No member identities appear in the catalog.

## M02 — Availability is not a promise

Member: “The word Available beside the picture made me think I could collect next Saturday. Then I discovered it meant free right now.”

Maya: “You need the requested interval. A future reservation and something sitting on the shelf are not the same fact. I can tell you that an interval isn't available without telling you who booked it.”

**FX-R02:** distinguish catalog presence from availability for a member-selected interval. For an interval whose interpretation has been agreed for the exercise, show available, unavailable or unable-to-determine outcomes without identifying another borrower. Unknown availability is neither an affirmative promise nor proof that another reservation exists. Availability can change before confirmation; booking remains governed by KD-ST-001, not this display. No reservation/hold is created by browsing. Equipment condition and date/time qualification remain FX-Q01/03; do not infer that every listed item is bookable.

## M03 — My bookings and the missed collection

Member: “I want to check the item and dates I actually booked. If I cancelled something, I don't want it to look as though it's still waiting for me. If I collected it, I need the actual loan, not a new copy of the booking.”

Maya: “Don't label confirmed reservations Approved or Pending. And a member who didn't arrive is not automatically a cancelled booking until we decide the no-show policy.”

**FX-R03:** extension own-booking list and detail show the requesting member's item, requested interval and known lifecycle outcome: confirmed, cancelled through a completed cancellation, or collected with a link to the actual loan. These are information concepts, not mandated status strings or an exhaustive state model. A member with no bookings sees that distinct from an unavailable list. Another member's booking detail cannot be read by changing a record reference, including cancelled/collected records. A member is not offered another member's cancellation operation. No inferred auto-expiry, editing, rebooking, staff approval or history-retention duration follows. KD-ST-001 already provides own confirmation; this premise extends retrieval/listing, not its booking criteria.

## M04 — My loan and a problem during use

Member: “The drill started making a noise. I want the desk to know before I bring it back. I shouldn't have to mark it returned just to tell them. And once I've handed it back I want to see that the desk recorded receipt, even if they are still assessing it.”

Maya: “Please show that it's a member report until we inspect it. I don't want an online note to close a physical loan.”

**FX-R09:** a member can view only their own actual loans, distinguish outstanding from returned, and see the item, linked booking, recorded handover and recorded return when present. Show a due obligation only when established under an explicitly supplied policy; an invented overdue calculation is not part of this premise. No loan is shown as collected merely because its booking was confirmed. Receipt is not a statement of fault, completed condition review or future bookability. Staff-only contact information about other borrowers and internal condition assessment notes are not part of this own-loan view. What further member-facing condition outcome is appropriate remains FX-Q09/10.

**FX-R18:** a member may submit a problem description against their own outstanding loan. A completed submission is visible as a report on that loan and does not record a return, close/extend the loan, decide fault or cancel any reservation. Another member cannot read or submit on it by substituting a loan reference. A returned loan is not eligible for this outstanding-loan reporting interaction; the member is told it is no longer outstanding rather than shown success for a new report. Character limits, attachments, duplicates, editing and urgent safety instructions are not supplied. FX-Q13 blocks any claim of monitored emergency response.
