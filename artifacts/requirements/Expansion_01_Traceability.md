### Qualified source wording — FX-R04

**Authored-source discrepancy, separate from FX-C01–06:** FX-SRC-04 § P01, FX-R04 says a collected booking's “actual loan remains outstanding.” Read unconditionally, this contradicts a received loan's returned state under FX-SRC-03 § O04, FX-R11/12 and KD-ST-009 AC-01. Counterexample: cancellation is attempted after collection and recorded return. FX-SRC-04 § P02, FX-R05 preserves actual loans; refusal must leave the returned loan returned, not assert outstanding. KD-ST-004 AC-03 already correctly preserves existing loan state. Its FX-R04 citation supports refusal of collected-booking cancellation, not an unconditional outstanding-state claim. This is source reconciliation for the candidate draft, not invented stakeholder policy or precedence based on document recency. Original sources and story criteria remain unchanged.
