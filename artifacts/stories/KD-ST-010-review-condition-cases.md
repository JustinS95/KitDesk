# Assess an item condition case

Local reference: **KD-ST-010**. Candidate pending user review, version 1.
Maturity: **provisional—specific clarification needed** for actual reviewer authority, assessment evidence and availability consequences.

## Summary and value

**As a** designated condition reviewer in this fixture, **I want to** assess a specific open condition case and retain or clear its restriction, **so that** observed concerns get a recorded outcome rather than disappear when an item is received back.

A desk coordinator who noted casing damage is not thereby authorized to clear it. Clearing that case is not “Mark available”. Evidence: [FX-SRC-08 § C02–C03](../../sources/expansion-01/08-condition-return-workshop.md), [FX-SRC-05 § D06](../../sources/expansion-01/05-text-design-revision.md).

## Scope and authority

FX-R14/15/20/21 are **author-defined synthetic premises, review pending**. The designated reviewer is a fictional task designation, not a production role or actual delegation. No safety certification, inspection checklist, repair, clearance reversal, contact directory, automatic reservation update or universal availability decision is introduced.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **AC-01 — Inspect condition-work context.** A separately designated fixture reviewer can inspect open condition cases with physical item, concern and relevant loan/receipt context for the assessment. Case access does not grant borrower-history/directory browsing or contact details merely because an item can be assessed. (FX-SRC-08 § C02, FX-R14; FX-SRC-05 § D06.)
- [ ] **AC-02 — Record a specific assessment.** For a selected open case, the designated reviewer can record an assessment and either retain that case's restriction with a reason or clear that case with an assessment outcome. The result remains attached to that physical item/case, not a different loan selected by a changed reference; separate/new concerns are not cleared together. (FX-SRC-08 § C03, FX-R15.)
- [ ] **AC-03 — Do not equate clearance with availability.** Clearing the case does not erase the concern, reopen a returned loan, certify safety, amend membership or automatically restore/cancel a later reservation. Opening the case is not assessment sign-off; clearance is not proof of canonical bookability or completion of any required inspection interval. (FX-SRC-08 § C03, FX-R15; FX-Q03/08.)
- [ ] **AC-04 — Enforce reviewer and current-state boundaries.** An ordinary coordinator's attempted clearance is refused and the case remains unchanged unless that actor is separately designated. Member, desk or manager titles alone grant no reviewer designation; members cannot read internal assessments or clear cases, including for an item they returned, even through bypassed controls. A case already cleared is not cleared again through the open-case interaction; show the known current state. Desk actors may see only related operational status for their lending task without gaining clearance. (FX-SRC-08 § C02–C03, FX-R14/15; FX-SRC-03 § O05, FX-R20; SRC-06 § A01.)
- [ ] **AC-05 — Qualify completion.** A known refusal/failure before completion leaves the case unchanged and does not claim an assessment outcome. An uncertain completion is not shown as success or unchanged state; reconcile the selected case's known outcome before inviting potentially duplicate condition handling. (FX-SRC-06 § X05, FX-R21.)

## Dependencies versus readiness

KD-ST-009 supplies a damaged-receipt case. Reviewing that record is independently useful after receipt; recording a return does not wait for clearance. FX-R14 permits bounded exercise examples but does not resolve real access. FX-Q03/08 block full operational authority/assessment and downstream availability, not expressing the two supported fixture outcomes.
